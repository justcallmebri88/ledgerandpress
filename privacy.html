Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Ledger & Press (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a live personalization and event branding studio based in Houston, Texas. This policy explains what information we collect through ledgerandpress.com (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit our inquiry form, we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, event date, event location, type of event, estimated number of guests, anticipated investment (if you choose to share it), the services you are interested in, and anything you tell us about your project. If you email us directly, we receive whatever you include in your message.

Information collected automatically. The Site does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics tools. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in server logs for security and reliability.

Try the Press. Names or text you type into the Try the Press preview stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare a proposal or quote.
  • To plan, book and deliver your event or project.
  • To communicate with you about your booking, including scheduling, design proofs and invoices.
  • To keep the Site secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the Site and our business. They handle your information only to provide their services to us:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers inquiry form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site.
  • Etsy, if you choose to shop with us there. Purchases on Etsy are covered by Etsy’s own privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business.

Event photos

We sometimes photograph our work, our station and finished pieces at events to share in our portfolio and on social media. If you would prefer that your event, your guests or your pieces not be featured, just let us know before or after your event and we will honor your request.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and booking information for as long as we need it to serve you and to meet our business, tax and legal obligations, and then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing ledgerandpress@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website, email or internet transmission is completely secure. Please do not send payment card numbers or other sensitive information through the inquiry form.

Children

The Site is intended for adults planning events and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Links to other sites

The Site links to other websites, such as our Etsy shop. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email ledgerandpress@gmail.com.
